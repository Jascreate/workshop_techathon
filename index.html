What if AI could do more than answer?

Traditional AI often responds to a question. Agentic AI systems can go further: they can work toward a goal, plan steps, use tools, and adapt their actions based on results.

This workshop introduces you to the concepts behind AI agents through practical examples, guided demonstrations, and project-oriented learning.

What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to systems that can work toward goals by planning steps, using tools, and taking actions with varying levels of human oversight.
