QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to systems that can work toward goals by planning steps, using tools, and taking actions with varying levels of human oversight.

Do I need prior AI experience?

No prior AI experience is required for this workshop on Agentic AI Systems.

When and where is the workshop?

The workshop will take place in Computer Lab 4 of ARSD College on 5th November, from 10:30 a.m. onwards.
